12.3 Capstone: Analytics dashboard

Phase 4's storytelling principles, made real for an actual audience — because a finding nobody can see doesn't count as delivered.

TL;DR
Build a dashboard that answers one specific, real business question clearly — not a general "explore the data" tool. Apply 4.1's exploratory-vs-explanatory distinction directly: this is explanatory work, so it should show the few charts that carry the actual finding, not everything you looked at during EDA. If your Phase 1 pipeline capstone (12.1) produced a gold-layer table, build this dashboard directly on top of it — the two capstones are meant to connect.

What to build

  • One specific business question your dashboard answers clearly — "which product categories are declining and why" beats "explore our sales data."
  • A data source ideally connected to your 12.1 pipeline's gold layer (6.5) — if you didn't build 12.1, any clean, modeled dataset works.
  • Chart types chosen deliberately per 4.2's decision framework — not defaulted to whatever your tool suggests first.
  • At least one annotation or callout (4.3) that states the finding directly, not just a chart the viewer has to interpret unaided.
  • A conscious check against 4.4's pitfalls — verify your axes aren't misleadingly truncated, and that you haven't accidentally created a Simpson's paradox situation by aggregating over a hidden confounding variable.

Track your progress

What "done" looks like

A working dashboard (Power BI, Tableau, or even a well-built set of charts in a notebook) plus a short README stating the specific question it answers, the finding it reveals, and a deliberate note on one chart-selection or truncation decision you made and why — demonstrating you're applying 4.2 and 4.4's principles consciously, not by accident.

Common places this goes wrong

  • A dashboard with no specific question. "General exploration" tools are exploratory (4.1) by definition — this capstone specifically wants explanatory, finding-first work.
  • Every chart type used just to show range. Recall 4.2 — chart choice should follow from what you're actually trying to show, not from a desire to demonstrate variety.
  • No connection back to the pipeline capstone. If you built 12.1, this dashboard sitting on hand-copied CSVs instead of your actual gold layer misses the point of the track being one connected chain.
