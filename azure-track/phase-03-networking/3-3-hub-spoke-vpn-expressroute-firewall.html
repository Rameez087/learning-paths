3.3 Hub and spoke, VPN, ExpressRoute and Firewall

Real organisations connect many networks. Hub and spoke is the common shape, and a few services build the links.

TL;DR
In hub and spoke, a central hub VNet holds shared services (firewall, gateways, DNS), and workload spoke VNets peer to it. VNet peering connects networks privately. VPN Gateway links over the internet with encryption, ExpressRoute is a private circuit through a provider, and Azure Firewall inspects and filters traffic centrally. Virtual WAN manages large hub networks for you.

The shape

Spokes talk to each other and to on premises through the hub, where traffic can be inspected.
ServiceUse it toWatch out for
VNet peeringConnect two VNets with low latencyNot transitive by default. Spoke to spoke needs routing through the hub
VPN GatewayEncrypted tunnel to on premises over the internetThroughput limits by SKU, takes time to deploy
ExpressRoutePrivate, predictable connection through a providerCost and lead time, plan a backup path
Azure FirewallCentral filtering, FQDN rules, loggingMeaningful fixed cost, needs user defined routes to steer traffic
Azure BastionBrowser based RDP and SSH without public IPsPriced per hour, needs its own subnet
Virtual WANManaged hubs for many branches and VNetsDifferent operating model from hand built hubs
Transitive routing
Peering A to hub and B to hub does not let A reach B. You add routing through a firewall or gateway in the hub with user defined routes, or use Virtual WAN which handles it.

Choose the link

A small team needs an encrypted link from one office to Azure at modest bandwidth.
VPN is the cheaper, faster way to start.
A bank needs predictable latency and private connectivity to Azure.
ExpressRoute is a private circuit and gives predictable performance.
Admins need to reach VMs without exposing RDP or SSH to the internet.
Bastion proxies sessions over TLS from the portal.
