5.3 Policy, Defender and security posture

Rules that enforce themselves beat documents that ask nicely. Policy prevents, Defender detects and advises.

TL;DR
Azure Policy evaluates resources against rules and can deny, audit, or modify and deploy settings automatically. Group policies into initiatives and assign them at a management group. Microsoft Defender for Cloud assesses your configuration (posture management) and offers threat protection for specific services. Both are central to landing zones (9.3).
EffectWhat it doesExample
DenyBlocks non compliant create or updateOnly allow approved regions
AuditFlags non compliance without blockingReport storage accounts allowing public blobs
ModifyChanges properties on create or updateAdd a required tag
DeployIfNotExistsDeploys a related resource when missingInstall diagnostic settings

Rolling out a policy safely

1. Start in audit mode

See what would be affected before blocking anyone.

2. Review the results

Fix or exempt legitimate exceptions with documented reasons.

3. Switch to deny

Communicate the change, then enforce at the right scope.

4. Remediate existing resources

Policy mostly acts on new changes. Remediation tasks fix existing ones for modify and deploy effects.

Policy is not RBAC
RBAC answers who can act. Policy answers what a resource is allowed to look like, whoever acts. Use both.

Defender for Cloud gives a secure score and recommendations across subscriptions. Its paid plans add protection for servers, databases, storage, containers and more. Check what each plan covers and costs before enabling widely.

Policy or RBAC?

Nobody, including owners, may create storage accounts with public blob access.
Policy enforces resource properties regardless of who deploys.
Only the data team may read a specific storage account.
Access to data is an identity question.

Check your understanding

1. Which effect blocks non compliant deployments?
Deny prevents the create or update.
2. Why start a new policy in audit mode?
It avoids breaking teams unexpectedly.
3. What is Defender for Cloud's posture feature?
It scores and recommends security improvements.
