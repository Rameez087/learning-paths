8.5 Prebuilt AI services versus LLMs

Not every AI task needs a large language model. Prebuilt services can be cheaper, faster and more predictable.

TL;DR
Azure AI services include Vision, Speech, Language, Translator and Document Intelligence for extracting structured fields from documents. They return consistent, specialised results at predictable cost. LLMs are flexible and handle open ended tasks but cost more per call and vary in output. Many solutions combine them: extract with a specialised service, reason with an LLM.
TaskPrebuilt serviceLLM
Read invoice fieldsDocument Intelligence, strong and consistentPossible, check accuracy and cost
Transcribe audioSpeech serviceSome models can, check latency and price
Translate fixed text at scaleTranslatorPossible, costlier
Summarise or answer open questionsLimitedStrong fit
Classify with a fixed label setLanguage service or small modelWorks, often overkill
Cost and determinism
A prebuilt service with a fixed price per page and stable output is easier to budget and audit than an LLM call whose tokens and answers vary.

Choose

Extract total and date from thousands of identical receipts.
A specialised extractor is cheaper and more consistent.
Answer free form questions over internal policy documents.
Open ended reasoning fits LLMs.
