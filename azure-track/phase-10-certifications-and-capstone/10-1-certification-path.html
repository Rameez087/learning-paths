10.1 Certification path

Certifications are a structured curriculum and a signal. Choose by role, and check what is current, because Microsoft retires and replaces exams often.

TL;DR
Microsoft certifications are role based. Common starting points are fundamentals (AZ-900 for cloud, DP-900 for data), then role exams such as administrator, developer, data engineer or architect. Exam lineups change often: several older exams have been retired in 2025 and 2026 and replaced. Always confirm on the official certification page before you study or book.

As of the research behind this page (October 2026), several exams had been retired or replaced, including DP-100, AI-102, AI-900, AZ-204 and AZ-500, with new AI, MLOps and security exams introduced. This list may already be out of date. Use the Microsoft Learn certification catalogue as the source of truth.

How to study well

  • Do the free Microsoft Learn paths and take the free practice assessment if one exists.
  • Build something for each domain in the Practice projects page. Hands on beats reading.
  • Read the official skills measured document and tick each line.
  • Book the exam to create a deadline, but check retake and renewal policies.
