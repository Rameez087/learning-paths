10.2 Practice projects

Seven small builds that each exercise a phase. Keep each in its own resource group and delete it when finished.

TL;DR
Do these in order. Each fits in a free account or a small budget if you clean up. Put every project in Git, deploy with Bicep or Terraform, and write a short README with a diagram.

Project checklist

Tick off as you complete each one. Progress is saved in your browser.

Clean up
Deleting the resource group stops the charges. Set a budget alert before you start, and check the portal for anything left running.

Write about each build: what you chose, what you tried and rejected, what broke. That write up is what makes the project worth showing in an interview.

Check your understanding

1. What is the safest way to stop charges after a practice project?
Resources in the group are removed.
2. What should every project include?
It makes the work reviewable.
3. What makes a project interview ready?
It shows judgement.
