4.1 Storage accounts and Data Lake Gen2

A storage account is the front door to blobs, files, queues and tables. Turn on the hierarchical namespace and it becomes a data lake.

TL;DR
A storage account holds blob containers, file shares, queues and tables. Choose the redundancy (LRS, ZRS, GRS and variants) and the access tier (hot, cool, cold, archive). Enabling the hierarchical namespace gives Azure Data Lake Storage Gen2: real directories, atomic renames and POSIX style ACLs, which analytics engines depend on.

Redundancy

OptionCopiesProtects against
LRS3 in one datacentreHardware failure
ZRS3 across zones in a regionA datacentre or zone failure
GRSLRS plus 3 in a paired regionA regional disaster, with asynchronous replication
GZRSZRS plus a paired regionBoth zone and regional failures

Read access variants (RA-GRS, RA-GZRS) let you read from the secondary. Replication to the secondary region is asynchronous, so a failover can lose recent writes.

Access tiers

TierStorage costAccess costUse for
HotHighestLowestActive data
CoolLowerHigherData read rarely, kept 30+ days
ColdLower stillHigher stillRarely read, kept 90+ days
ArchiveLowestHighest, hours to rehydrateLong term retention

Lifecycle management policies move or delete blobs automatically based on age. Early deletion fees apply if you move data out of cool, cold or archive too soon. Check current minimum durations.

Why the hierarchical namespace matters

Analytics jobs rename directories constantly when committing output, so the hierarchical namespace makes them fast and safe.
Security basics
Prefer Entra ID authentication with RBAC roles over account keys and SAS tokens. Disable shared key access if your tools allow it, turn off anonymous blob access, and use private endpoints for sensitive data.

Choose the setting

Raw files for Spark pipelines with heavy directory renames.
Gen2 gives atomic directory operations that analytics engines rely on.
Compliance data kept seven years, almost never read.
Archive is cheapest for long retention, with slow retrieval.
A business critical dataset must survive losing a whole region.
Only geo redundant options replicate to a second region.

Check your understanding

1. What does the hierarchical namespace add?
It turns blob storage into Data Lake Storage Gen2.
2. Is geo replication synchronous?
The secondary region lags the primary.
3. Preferred way to authorise access?
Identity based access is auditable and revocable.
