1.4 The 2026 FDE landscape

224 open FDE roles across 39 AI companies, as of mid-2026 — and the market has shifted from pure AI-FOMO hiring toward demanding proven ROI.

TL;DR
FDE (or equivalent-titled) roles are live at Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Databricks, Cohere, and a growing list of well-funded AI startups. Compensation varies enormously by company type: Palantir's median historically sits around $167K, while AI labs pay a 60-150% premium over that, reflecting both an AI-literacy premium and competitive pressure among labs racing to lock down deployment talent. New York has surpassed San Francisco as the primary hiring hub for these roles in 2026 (35% of postings vs. 11%).

The shift from AI-FOMO to proven-ROI hiring

The 2024/2025 hiring wave was driven by companies fearing they'd fall behind on AI adoption. The 2026 market has shifted: companies now pay FDE-level premiums specifically for engineers who can demonstrate a direct link between their deployments and customer retention — this reframes Phase 10's "prove impact" material from a nice-to-have communication skill into the literal thing the market is now paying for.

Two industry-scale bets, launched the same month

In May 2026, OpenAI launched "The Deployment Company" — a separately incorporated FDE business backed by over $4B in investor funding from firms including TPG, Bain Capital, and Brookfield. Days later, Anthropic announced a $1.5B joint venture with Blackstone and Goldman Sachs to embed Claude FDEs specifically inside financial-services customers. When two of the largest AI labs in the world spin up dedicated, separately-incorporated FDE businesses within the same month, the role has stopped being a tactical hire and become a strategic category in its own right.

What the job descriptions consistently ask for

Across FDE postings at OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Cohere, a consistent pattern emerges: strong production software engineering (Python at minimum, often Rust/Go/TypeScript for platform-side work), real experience with LLMs in production — prompting, evaluation, agent design, retrieval systems, not just having used the API casually — the ability to communicate with both technical and business stakeholders in the same meeting, and genuine comfort with ambiguity and travel, since the role often involves being on-site with a customer for weeks at a time.
