5.1 Data-centric AI and data quality

Most of machine learning research improves models. A growing field improves the data. That is where your daily work already lives.

TL;DR
Data-centric AI treats the data as the thing to engineer and improve, holding the model fixed. Zha and colleagues organise the field into three goals: training data development, inference (evaluation) data development and data maintenance. Data quality problems you fix in pipelines every week are research questions here.

The three goals

Goals of data-centric AI

Collecting, labelling, cleaning, augmenting and selecting the data a model learns from. Research asks which defects matter, how to detect them automatically and how to repair them cheaply.

Your hook: you clean and transform messy enterprise data every day.

Building test sets and benchmarks that expose what models get wrong. A benchmark with duplicates, leakage or label errors gives false confidence.

Your hook: building trustworthy test data is your reproduction and benchmark path.

Keeping data healthy over time: monitoring drift, versioning, refreshing and tracing lineage. Pipelines break quietly when upstream data changes.

Your hook: production pipelines, lineage and drift are your territory.

Whang and colleagues survey data collection and quality challenges for deep learning, covering acquisition, labelling, cleaning and the interaction between data quality and model robustness. Both papers are good entry points for building your literature map (2.2).

Which goal is it?

Detecting mislabelled rows in a training set before fitting a model.
Cleaning and label repair improve the data the model learns from.
Building a held out test set with documented construction and no overlap with training.
Constructing a trustworthy test set is evaluation data development.
Monitoring a feature's distribution and alerting when upstream data changes.
Ongoing monitoring and upkeep belongs to data maintenance.

Starter questions

  • Which data defects (duplicates, missing values, label noise, staleness) hurt model quality most, and by how much?
  • Can data defects be detected automatically on enterprise style tables?
  • How do cleaning choices change downstream model fairness or accuracy?
  • What is the cheapest data change that gives the largest quality gain?

Check your understanding

1. What does data-centric AI hold fixed while improving results?
The focus shifts from changing the model to improving the data.
2. Which is evaluation data development?
Creating trustworthy test data is one of the three goals.
3. Why is this field a strong fit for a data engineer?
The field's research tasks overlap closely with everyday pipeline work.

Sources: Data-centric AI: Perspectives and Challenges (Zha et al.); Data Collection and Quality Challenges in Deep Learning (Whang et al.)
