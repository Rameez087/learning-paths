5.4 Provenance, lineage and ML systems

When existing lineage tools hit a practical limit, that gap is where systems research starts.

TL;DR
Lineage records where data came from and how it was derived, at table, column or row level. Finer granularity is more useful and harder to capture. ML systems research asks what breaks when pipelines retrain models on changing data. Sculley and colleagues showed that data dependencies are a major source of hidden technical debt in ML systems.

Lineage granularity

Column level is where open integrations often have gaps for custom transformations.

Research angles

How completely do OpenLineage style integrations capture column level lineage for PySpark transformations, joins and UDFs? Benchmark against known ground truth on public or synthetic pipelines.

How much overhead does finer granularity add, and can sampling or static analysis reduce it?

Can lineage help debug data defects faster, or find which upstream change broke a model? Measure time to root cause with and without it.

ML systems questions

  • What breaks when a pipeline retrains a model on drifting data, and how soon do monitors notice?
  • Which data dependencies create hidden coupling between models and upstream systems (Sculley et al.)?
  • How should pipelines validate data before training to avoid silent failures?
Protect your employer
If you built a lineage tool at work, do not publish it or any client details without permission. A public benchmark on synthetic PySpark pipelines gives you the same research question with no confidentiality risk.

Which granularity?

You need to know which source column contributed to a wrong figure in a dashboard measure.
Job or table level shows which pipeline ran, not which column caused the error.

Check your understanding

1. What does column level lineage record?
It traces derivations at the column level, finer than table or job lineage.
2. Why does finer lineage granularity cost more?
Capturing derivation inside transformations needs deeper instrumentation or analysis.
3. How can you study lineage without exposing employer data?
Public or synthetic data keeps the research question and removes confidentiality risk.

Sources: Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems (Sculley et al.); OpenLineage; Data-centric AI: Perspectives and Challenges (Zha et al.)
