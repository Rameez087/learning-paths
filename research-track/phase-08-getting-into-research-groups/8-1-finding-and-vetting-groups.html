8.1 Finding and vetting research groups

Good outreach starts long before the email. You are looking for a group whose recent work matches yours and which is plausibly able to take someone new.

TL;DR
Find groups by topic, not prestige: search recent papers on your question, note the groups that keep appearing, then read their pages. Vet each on three things: recent output, fit with your background and signs they take new people. Shortlist 8 to 12. The list below is a starting map, not a guarantee.

Vetting checklist for each group

Which target is better?

Group A is famous but has no paper near your topic in five years. Group B is smaller with three recent papers on data quality for ML.
Fit and recency beat fame. A precise question about recent work is what gets replies.

Check your understanding

1. How should you find groups to contact?
Topical fit makes your outreach relevant.
2. Why shortlist 8 to 12 groups?
Response rates are modest, so a focused but adequate list is wise.
3. What should you do before writing to any group?
Specifics show you did the work.
