8.5 Applications: CV, statement and deadlines

If your goal includes a research master's or PhD, the artifact you build here becomes the centre of your application.

TL;DR
A research focused application is a one or two page CV with projects first, a statement of purpose that tells one coherent story from your experience to a specific research question, and references who can speak to your work. List every deadline in week 4 of the plan. Many programmes decide months ahead, so apply with work in progress if needed.

The three documents

One or two pages. Lead with projects and outputs (the repo, the report), then experience, then education. Use numbers and links. Cut anything that does not support the research story.

Structure: a specific question you care about, the experience that led you to it, the artifact that shows you can do research, why this group or programme, and what you plan to work on. Avoid childhood stories and generic praise.

People who can describe your work concretely: a manager, a collaborator from an open community, a professor who saw your report. Ask early and give them your artifact and a short summary.

Deadlines
Many Fall intakes for Erasmus Mundus, German and Italian programmes have deadlines between roughly December and spring of the preceding year. I have not verified individual deadlines. Check every programme's official page now, put dates in a table and work backwards.
