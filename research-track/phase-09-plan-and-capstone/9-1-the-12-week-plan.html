9.1 The 12 week plan

A plan you can tick off. Emails go out in week 7, once you have a result to show.

TL;DR
Four phases over 12 weeks: read and choose (weeks 1 to 2), reproduce (3 to 6), extend and reach out (7 to 9), talk and decide (10 to 12). Outreach begins only in the third phase. Every earlier phase exists to make that first email worth reading.
The highlighted phase is where outreach begins.

Weeks 1 to 2: read and choose

Weeks 3 to 6: reproduce

Weeks 7 to 9: extend and reach out

Weeks 10 to 12: talk and decide

Timing
Inboxes in Europe tend to slow around the year end, so aim for first emails before mid December if your timeline allows. Adjust the weeks to start from your own start date.

Check your understanding

1. When should you send your first emails?
Evidence makes the email worth reading.
2. What should you do if you fall behind?
A finished smaller project beats an unfinished large one.
3. Why start deadline tracking in week 1 or 2?
Early tracking prevents missing windows.
