9.3 Capstone: your outreach package

The finish line for this track: everything you need to send with confidence, and a self review before you press send.

TL;DR
Your outreach package has five parts: a published artifact (repo and report), a one page CV with projects first, a shortlist of vetted groups with one precise question each, a tailored email for each, and a call preparation sheet. When every box below is ticked, you are ready.

The package

Tick each item only when you can point to the actual file or link.

Self review rubric

Could a stranger rerun your main table in under an hour? Do the numbers carry intervals? Is the gap log honest?

For each group, can you name two recent papers and one specific link to your work? Would they recognise your question as theirs?

Is each email under about 200 words, with one question answerable in a line and a small next step?

Is anything confidential in the repo or report? Have you checked history, notebooks and screenshots?

Everything downstream depends on the artifact on the left.
After the package
Keep going. Choose Project B or C, write a workshop paper, keep contributing in an open community, and keep your reading log alive. Research is a habit, not a single project.

Check your understanding

1. When is your outreach package ready?
Evidence is tangible, so verify each part.
2. Which item guards against confidentiality problems?
Sensitive data can hide in history, notebooks and screenshots.
3. What should you do after sending the package?
Research output compounds, so keep going.

Sources: How to Email a Potential Supervisor (Academic Positions); NeurIPS Paper Checklist; Improving Reproducibility in ML Research (Pineau et al.)
