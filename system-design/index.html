One app.
Ten breaking points.

You're the engineer behind Chirp, a Twitter-style app. It launches on one server. Then it grows — and breaks, again and again, in exactly the order real systems do. Every concept in this track exists because something you built actually failed first. No abstract theory before the pain that motivates it.
